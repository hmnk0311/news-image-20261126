## Lead — US tariffs proposed on 14 partners

- Reuters, Al Jazeera and the Financial Express rate table: after an
  eight-month Section 301 probe into 16 partners, USTR proposed
  tariffs on 14 — China, Bangladesh, Cambodia, the EU, India,
  Indonesia, Japan, South Korea, Malaysia, Singapore, Switzerland,
  Taiwan, Thailand and Vietnam. Mexico and Norway were spared.
- Rates as proposed: Vietnam up to 35%; India up to 25% (17% with a
  deal); China +20% on top of roughly 25% first-term duties;
  Singapore up to 20%; Bangladesh, Cambodia, Indonesia, Malaysia and
  Thailand up to 18%; the EU 15%; Japan, South Korea, Switzerland
  and Taiwan up to 15%. Hearings are set for 25 January 2027; nothing
  is yet in force.
- Reuters: Brazil faces a separate Section 301 proposal — 10% on
  finished Brazilian aircraft and extra duties on sugar over quota.
- Reuters (markets, Wednesday): Nasdaq fell 2% on Wednesday, down
  almost 20% from early October's record; the VIX is up 60% in
  November; the 10-year Treasury yield fell 6bp to 4.84%. US markets
  are closed Thursday for Thanksgiving.
- Reuters: Norway's foreign minister Eide welcomed the decision not
  to propose tariffs on Norwegian goods.
